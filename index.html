The projects

Two classics. One new home.

reDriver: Extended

In development
Driver 1, rebuilt as a native Windows program. It runs the original game’s own code on the reDriver 2: Extended engine, with no emulator.
  • Installs from your own PlayStation disc, with nothing included
  • Native 64-bit, with the same launcher, options, pad support and sound as Driver 2
  • The original front end, missions and handling, plus the intro movies
  • Save games and mission progress
  • Widescreen, smooth frame rates and a far view option
  • Options page in the launcher

reDriver 2: Extended

Beta 0.4.1 out now

Driver 2 with the doors open. It keeps the original game and adds what the PlayStation never had.

  • Split screen for up to 8 players, plus pads, wheels and the Steam Deck
  • More cities and cars: the Driver 1 cities, Miami and Newcastle, and Gran Turismo cars
  • Rain, wet roads, day and night, and busier traffic and cops
  • Taxi jobs, street races, a CB radio and car customizing
  • A level editor, mission maker, photo mode and Car Creator
  • Modding tools and Lua scripts for making your own missions, cars and mods
  • 21 languages
Get playing

A simpler way to play

1. Download the launcher
One lightweight application handles installation and updates for both games.
2. Use your own game discs
Point the launcher to your own game disc images.
3. Let it install
The launcher extracts and installs the required game data on your computer, and keeps it up to date in place.
4. Play
Your games are installed and ready to play. No complicated setup, no extra steps.

The launcher does not include or distribute game data. You'll need your own legally obtained game disc images to install each game. All game data is extracted and generated locally on your computer and is never distributed by this project.
